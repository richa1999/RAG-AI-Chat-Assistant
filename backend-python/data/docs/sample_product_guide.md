# Product User Guide - SmartHome Hub

## Introduction

The SmartHome Hub is an intelligent home automation system that connects and controls all your smart devices from a single interface.

## Getting Started

### Initial Setup

1. Unbox your SmartHome Hub and connect it to power
2. Download the SmartHome app from the App Store or Google Play
3. Create an account or sign in
4. Follow the in-app setup wizard

### Connecting Devices

The hub supports over 500 different smart devices including:
- Smart lights (Philips Hue, LIFX, Nanoleaf)
- Thermostats (Nest, Ecobee, Honeywell)
- Security cameras (Ring, Arlo, Wyze)
- Smart locks (August, Schlage, Yale)
- Voice assistants (Alexa, Google Assistant)

## Features

### Voice Control

Activate voice control by saying "Hey SmartHome" followed by your command:
- "Turn off living room lights"
- "Set temperature to 72 degrees"
- "Show front door camera"

### Automation Rules

Create custom automation rules in the app:
1. Go to Automation tab
2. Tap "Create New Rule"
3. Set triggers (time, device state, location)
4. Set actions (what devices should do)
5. Save and enable

### Energy Monitoring

Track your home's energy usage in real-time:
- Daily, weekly, and monthly reports
- Device-by-device breakdown
- Cost estimates based on your utility rates
- Suggestions for energy savings

## Troubleshooting

### Hub Won't Connect to WiFi

1. Ensure you're using 2.4GHz WiFi (5GHz not supported)
2. Check that your password is correct
3. Move hub closer to router
4. Restart hub by unplugging for 30 seconds

### Device Not Responding

1. Check device has power
2. Verify device is within 50 feet of hub
3. Try removing and re-adding the device
4. Update device firmware in the app
