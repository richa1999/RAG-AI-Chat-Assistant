# Company Policies - TechCorp Inc.

## Employee Handbook

### Work Hours and Attendance

**Standard Work Hours:**
- Full-time employees: 40 hours per week
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (must be available)
- Flexible start time: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM
- Flexible end time: 3:00 PM - 7:00 PM

**Remote Work Policy:**
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work requirements:
- Reliable high-speed internet connection
- Dedicated workspace
- Available during core hours
- Attend all required meetings

### Time Off

**Paid Time Off (PTO):**
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year
- 6+ years: 25 days per year
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance

**Holidays:**
TechCorp observes 10 paid holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve

**Sick Leave:**
- Unlimited sick days (within reason)
- No doctor's note required for absences under 3 days
- Notify manager as soon as possible

### Benefits

**Health Insurance:**
- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Company pays 80% of premium for employee
- Company pays 50% of premium for dependents
- Coverage begins first day of employment

**Retirement:**
- 401(k) plan with 5% company match
- Immediate vesting
- Financial planning resources available

**Professional Development:**
- $2,000 annual budget for courses, conferences, or certifications
- Must be related to current role or career growth
- Submit request form to manager for approval

### Code of Conduct

**Professional Behavior:**
All employees must maintain professional conduct including:
- Respect for colleagues regardless of position
- No harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Honesty and integrity in all business dealings
- Protection of confidential information

**Dress Code:**
- Business casual Monday-Thursday
- Casual Friday
- Professional attire when meeting clients
- Use good judgment

**Technology Usage:**
- Company equipment for business purposes
- Limited personal use acceptable during breaks
- No illegal downloads or inappropriate content
- Email and internet usage may be monitored

### Performance Reviews

- Conducted annually in Q1
- Mid-year check-ins in Q3
- Self-assessment required
- Goals set for upcoming year
- Merit increases based on performance

### Termination Policy

**Voluntary Resignation:**
- 2 weeks notice required for non-management
- 4 weeks notice required for management
- Exit interview conducted
- Final paycheck includes unused PTO

**Involuntary Termination:**
- Progressive discipline (warning, written warning, final warning, termination)
- Immediate termination for gross misconduct
- Severance package may be offered based on tenure
